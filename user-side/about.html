About
Our Foundation

Jayasri Kannan Foundation is committed to social welfare and community development.

The Foundation brings together volunteers, healthcare professionals, educational institutions, charitable organisations and community members to support people through practical service initiatives.

Our programmes are designed around the needs of communities and focus on healthcare, education, youth development, awareness and social welfare.

Our Vision

To create a healthy, educated, empowered, and socially developed society by supporting people in need.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide healthcare, education, social welfare, environmental protection, and development support to poor, rural, vulnerable, and underserved communities.

Our Core
Values

Compassion

We believe every person deserves dignity, care and respect. Compassion is at the heart of all we do.

Service

Integrity
