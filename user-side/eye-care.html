MEGHA FREE EYE CHECK-UP CAMP

In association with participating educational institutions, healthcare professionals and hospital partners.

The MEGHA Free Eye Check-Up Camp is one of the flagship programmes of Jayasri Kannan Foundation. The camp provides free eye screening and eye-health awareness for students and community members.

We work with qualified ophthalmologists, optometrists and healthcare professionals to ensure quality eye care for all participants. Guidance for further consultation is provided where appropriate.
