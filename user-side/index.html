About
Our Foundation

Jayasri Kannan Foundation is committed to social welfare and community development.

The Foundation brings together volunteers, healthcare professionals, educational institutions, charitable organisations and community members to support people through practical service initiatives.

Our programmes are designed around the needs of communities and focus on healthcare, education, youth development, awareness and social welfare.

MEGHA FREE EYE CHECK-UP CAMP

In association with participating educational institutions, healthcare professionals and hospital partners.

The MEGHA Free Eye Check-Up Camp is one of the flagship programmes of Jayasri Kannan Foundation. The camp provides free eye screening and eye-health awareness for students and community members.

We work with qualified ophthalmologists, optometrists and healthcare professionals to ensure quality eye care for all participants. Guidance for further consultation is provided where appropriate.

Blood Donation

Every Drop
Counts

Voluntary blood donation can help patients who require blood and blood components. Jayasri Kannan Foundation supports blood donation awareness and community blood donation programmes.

We collaborate with appropriate hospitals, blood banks and service organisations to conduct safe and well-organised blood donation camps.

  • ✓Community mobilisation
  • ✓Blood donation camps
  • ✓Student awareness
  • ✓Volunteer participation
  • ✓Donor appreciation

"Every Donor Can Make a Difference."
