Dr. Jayasri Kannan

Dr. Jayasri Kannan

Ph.D. & M.Sc. in Nursing

Founder & Chairperson

Founder Profile

Dr. Jayasri Kannan is a nursing professional, educator, researcher, and healthcare advocate with 9 years of professional experience. She holds a Ph.D. in Nursing and an M.Sc. in Nursing with specialization in Medical-Surgical Nursing, Cardiovascular and Thoracic Nursing, along with a B.Sc. in Nursing.

She currently serves as an Assistant Professor and Research Supervisor at Tantia University, with experience in nursing education, clinical practice, research guidance, and healthcare-related academic activities. Her professional journey also includes experience as an Assistant Professor, Clinical Instructor, and Staff Nurse.

Dr. Jayasri has actively participated in health awareness programs, research conferences, workshops, and community-oriented initiatives covering areas such as cancer awareness, patient safety, COVID-19 prevention, AIDS awareness, and quality healthcare.

Her academic and professional achievements include being a First Rank Holder and Gold Medalist in M.Sc. Nursing at SRM IST, along with several academic awards, research presentations, and publications in areas including stroke recovery, hemodialysis care, breast cancer awareness, cervical cancer awareness, and nursing practice.

Through the Jayasri Kannan Foundation, her experience in healthcare, education, research, and awareness can be presented as the foundation for initiatives focused on serving communities, spreading health knowledge, supporting education, and contributing to a healthier society.

Our Foundation

Jayasri Kannan Foundation is committed to social welfare and community development.

The Foundation brings together volunteers, healthcare professionals, educational institutions, charitable organisations and community members to support people through practical service initiatives.

Our programmes are designed around the needs of communities and focus on healthcare, education, youth development, awareness and social welfare.
